I'm a scientific data manager at the Norwegian Meteorological Institute and the University Centre in Svalbard.

My motivation is to help transform how we work with data — from building data workflows and software, to helping people and projects publish and reuse data, and developing standardised approaches to documenting data.

Ultimately, I want to help maximise the real world impact that we can extract from environmental data.
