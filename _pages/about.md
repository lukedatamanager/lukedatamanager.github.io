<section class="about-page__section" id="about-me">
	<p>I am Luke Marsden, a scientific data manager working with the Norwegian Meteorological Institute and the University Centre in Svalbard.</p>

	<p>This site is a place for me to write about scientific data management in practice. That includes the day-to-day work of organising data well, building useful workflows, improving access to environmental datasets, and thinking more carefully about how data can support services and better decisions both inside and outside of academia.</p>

	<p>I am especially interested in the gap between producing scientific data and creating real-world impact from it. Good data management does not solve everything on its own, but it does make it much easier for data to be shared, integrated, reused, and turned into something that people can actually act on.</p>

	<p>Alongside blog posts, I also share practical tutorials. These include examples related to environmental data workflows and tasks such as creating CF-NetCDF files in Python.</p>

	<p>Views posted on this website are my own and are not necessarily shared by the institutions I work for, the projects I am part of, or anyone else for that matter.</p>
</section>
