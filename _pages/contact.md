I’m always interested in discussing ideas, sharing experiences and collaborating on projects that make better use of scientific data.

I’m also happy to give talks and presentations on environmental data management, FAIR data, and how research data can be made more useful beyond the projects that produce it.

Please note that I don’t have the time or funding to transform individual datasets or collections outside the projects I work on. I’m very happy to discuss ideas and approaches, but I hope you understand that I’m not able to take on standalone data transformation work.
